Flowers that meant trouble in 1884

In Kate Greenaway's Language of Flowers, yellow roses meant “Decrease of love. Jealousy.” Hydrangeas meant “A boaster. Heartlessness.” Peonies meant “Shame. Bashfulness.” Lavender meant “Distrust.” Nobody reads bouquets that way anymore, so order them freely.
