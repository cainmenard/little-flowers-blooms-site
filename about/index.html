About Hi, I'm Amelie.

I'm a sophomore at UL Lafayette, and I make wedding and event flowers out of Lafayette, Louisiana.

My first orders were corsages for friends and arrangements for their moms. Word got around, and now I make flowers for weddings, formals, showers and birthdays for families across Acadiana.

I make every piece myself, so you deal with the same person from the first message to delivery. I answer every message within a day, and every quote comes in writing before you pay anything.
