Questions brides ask

How far ahead should we book?

Reach out as soon as you have a date and a venue. Dates are held in the order deposits come in, and final counts lock 3 weeks before the wedding.

What do wedding flowers cost?

It depends on your wedding party, the flowers you pick and the season. Send your date and party size and I'll reply within 24 hours with a written quote that lists every piece.

How does payment work?

A 50% non-refundable deposit holds your date. The balance is due 21 days before the wedding.

What if a flower isn't available that week?

I swap in one of similar color, style and value, and tell you before the wedding.

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes. Delivery covers placing the altar pieces and helping pin boutonnieres. Teardown and vessel pickup after the reception is available too.

Which towns do you serve?

Lafayette, Youngsville, Broussard, Scott, Carencro and Breaux Bridge. Ask about other towns.
